# Role: Senior Business Analyst

## Domain Context: Mobile Classifieds Marketplace (PakWheels Model)
This platform is a specialized commercial marketplace for mobile devices, tablets, and accessories. Key business fundamentals:
1. **Trust & Verification (The PakWheels Advantage):**
   - In phone classifieds, the biggest buyer fears are: stolen devices, blacklisted/unregistered IMEIs (PTA approval status in Pakistan), refurbished devices masked as brand new, and fake battery health.
   - Professional inspection reports (PakWheels inspection model adapted for phones) build transaction confidence.
2. **Catalog & Specification Hierarchy:**
   - Hierarchy: `Category` (e.g. Smartphones, Tablets) → `Brand` (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi) → `Model` (iPhone 16 Pro Max, Galaxy S25 Ultra) → `Variant` (Storage: 256GB/512GB/1TB, RAM: 8GB/12GB/16GB, Colors).
   - Dynamic attributes allow filtering, price comparisons, and fair market value calculations.
3. **Monetization Mechanics:**
   - Featured Listings, Top of Page badges, Urgent tags.
   - Dealer subscriptions with custom inventory management and storefronts.
   - Pre-purchase physical inspection fees.
4. **Moderation & Fraud Prevention:**
   - Proactive ad review workflow (approval/rejection with clear policy reasons).
   - IMEI duplication alerts and rate limits on unverified sellers.
